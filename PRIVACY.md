\# Polar Uninstaller — Privacy Policy



\*\*Last Updated:\*\* November 15, 2026



Polar Uninstaller respects user privacy.



\- \*\*No Personal Data Collection:\*\* Polar Uninstaller does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, or information about installed applications.

\- \*\*Local Processing:\*\* Uninstallation, cleanup, file management, and storage analysis are performed locally on the user's device.

\- \*\*Update Checks:\*\* The application connects to GitHub to check for and download software updates.

\- \*\*Third-Party Services:\*\* GitHub may process technical connection information according to its own privacy policy.

\- \*\*Data Storage:\*\* Application settings may be stored locally on the user's device.



This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.
